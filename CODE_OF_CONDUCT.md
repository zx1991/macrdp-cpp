# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a focused, respectful place to discuss remote
desktop engineering. Participants must communicate with respect, assume good
faith while still checking technical claims, and avoid harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when behavior violates this standard. Report conduct concerns
through the private contact or reporting channel configured for this
repository, with the relevant link or context. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant and applies to project
spaces and discussions maintained by this repository.
